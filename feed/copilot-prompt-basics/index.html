Start with six simple words in Copilot

By Vishnupriya

A hundred prompts is a daunting place to begin, so don’t start there. Six verbs cover most of an office day: summarise, draft, rewrite, explain, compare and plan. Try one in Outlook, Word, Excel or Teams, add your own details, and review before you send. The full list is in the Knowledge Library when you’re ready for more.
