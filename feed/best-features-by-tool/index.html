AI Nugget · Topic 2

You don’t have to pick the “right” AI tool

By Vishnupriya

Choosing between ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot feels like a high-stakes decision, but it isn’t one. Each has an edge: ChatGPT for voice and work mode, Gemini for quick slides and Gmail, Claude for analysis, skills and automation, and Copilot for your Microsoft world. Use them all, send each job to the one that fits, and switch when free credits run out. The tools change, but your skill stays.
