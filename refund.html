Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Free sessions

Registering for a free session costs nothing. You can cancel any time, just let us know or remove the event from your calendar.

Membership (₹2,600 for 6 months, one-time)

  • Membership is a one-time payment that covers 6 months of access. It does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • If you change your mind before your access is activated, tell us and we will refund the full amount.
  • Once membership is activated and you have access to the members' content, the payment is non-refundable, because digital content is delivered immediately.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or you cannot access the content because of a problem on our side that we cannot fix, we will refund you in full.

How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email us with the name and email you signed up with. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within about 7 working days.

Session changes

If we cancel a session, you will be informed and any recording or material will be shared with members when available.
